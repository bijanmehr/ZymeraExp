The rethink — how the requirement changed the design

Near-perfect connectivity makes the team ONE body

With the graph required to hold ~every step, no agent goes anywhere alone — the team is one kinematic body with ten joints, and every move is a deformation of the whole. Two consequences reorganize everything: coverage = the body sweeping (a connected comb walking the map lane by lane — our boustrophedon oracle already proves ~80–90% coverage at 100% connectivity exists), and the failure mode = the body sitting — not fragmentation but the huddle, exactly what every per-step guardrail produced in our own history. Both huddle and comb satisfy the constraint; the entire game is which equilibrium the team finds. Division of labour therefore isn't scattered territories — it's structure on the body: lanes at the front, hinges at the folds, chains where terrain demands depth.

Objective discipline

Trained on the mission spec only

The lab stays reward-agnostic. The agent trains on (G, K) — coverage with connectivity as the hard mission requirement (the team in one piece), enforced softly via the Lagrangian dual (with the Hyper-Singularity floor as backstop) — and per-agent credit comes from marginal-contribution credit (difference rewards: a decomposition of the same G, zero new reward content). With connectivity in G, remove-i evaluation automatically pays the hinge — a relay's Dᵢ is everything its link enables — and auto-sizes the relay count. The free market moves from a congestion price (an implicit reward — now forbidden) to a claim feature in the KB: the "teammate wall-true closer?" column in stage 4. Bump · anti-overlap · congestion-as-reward: ablation arms only.

Pre-registered predictions: P1 flat credit reproduces the huddle (our own history, now as the control arm) · P2 marginal credit produces the sweep — coverage climbs toward the connected ceiling, redundancy → 1 · P3 hinge-holding appears at doorways with no role head, visible as Dᵢ spikes on the hinge agents · P4 the role-head arm adds nothing beyond the emergent roles (its ablation tests exactly that) · P5 on deep maps coverage saturates at the connected ceiling, validating the oracle. If P2 fails, the honest conclusion is that (G,K)+credit is insufficient at this scale — a finding, and we report it.
