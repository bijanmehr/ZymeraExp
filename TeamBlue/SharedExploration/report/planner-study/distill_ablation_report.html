MVProp distillation — ablation

The planner is a distilled value-iteration net; what in the recipe actually matters?

MVProp learns a passability field w=σ(conv[blocked,goal]+2) and floods a value V=max(r, γ·w·max₄(V)) for K sweeps, so the field is γ^dist — distilled to match the exact 4-connected wavefront. The deployed recipe is K=32, γ=0.90, trained on random rectangles. This ablates the three plausible levers — propagation depth K, discount γ, and the training map distribution — re-distilling each and re-scoring path success on six map families.

What matters

1 · γ pushed to 1 backfires — alone. At γ=0.99 the field γ^dist goes nearly flat, the distillation can't fit it (MSE 0.0009 → 0.057), and the greedy gradient washes out — every score drops (cave 96 → 59). γ=0.95 is the safe step, and it alone lifts MovingAI-room 31 → 69.

2 · More propagation depth K helps within-range routing. K=64 at γ=0.90 (same low MSE) lifts BSP-rooms 70 → 94 and caves/dao to 100 — K=32 wasn't fully flooding the reachable region. It can't fix perfect mazes, whose paths exceed the γ0.90 range.

3 · Training distribution matters — adding mazes/rooms/caves to the rectangles-only training lifts MovingAI-room 31 → 67 on its own. The corridor weakness was partly a distribution gap.

4 · The knobs compound. γ=0.99, which fails in isolation, becomes the best recipe paired with K=128 + mixed training: perfect-maze 10 → 29 (3×), BSP-rooms 70 → 100, MovingAI-maze 80 → 98. The extended range only pays off once there's enough depth and training signal to hold the flat field.

Verdict. The deployed recipe (K32, γ0.9, rectangles) is suboptimal on corridor and maze terrain — there's real headroom. A better recipe is more depth + diverse training + higher γ, together; the clean sweet spot to try next is γ0.95 + K64 + mixed (avoids the flat-field fitting problem) versus the aggressive γ0.99 + K128 + mixed. Perfect recursive-backtracker mazes stay the hardest case (best 29%) — their diameter can exceed even K=128.
Two honest caveats. (1) These are quick 2000-step distillations; the best combo's MSE (0.021) is well above the converged configs (0.001), so it is undertrained — full training would likely push it higher still. (2) Whether a better planner moves the mission is a separate question: the multi-agent coverage is belief-bound (agents plan on a sparse comms-fused map, not the full one), so most of this gain may not surface end-to-end — except on rooms/corridors, exactly where both the planner and the mission are weakest. That end-to-end check is the natural follow-up.
