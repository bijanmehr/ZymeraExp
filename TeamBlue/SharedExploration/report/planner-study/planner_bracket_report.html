The L1 planner, bracketed in the real team

Multi-agent env, 10 agents, harder terrain — every controller measured between a lower and an upper bound.

Earlier I tested the planners in isolation, one agent at a time — the wrong unit. Here they run inside the real 10-agent mission. The goal-assignment (L3) is held fixed (god-view Voronoi, the same targets for everyone), and only the L1 navigation is swapped, so the comparison isolates the planner and is bracketed on both sides: a lower bound (greedy — no pathfinding, freezes at walls) and an upper bound (god-view BFS — perfect pathfinding on the full map). The learned planners run on the real partial, comms-fused belief and should land in between.

What the bracket says

1 · The bracket width is terrain-dependent. On light clutter everyone piles near the top (greedy 65 ≈ planners ≈ upper 68): with good goals and constant re-assignment, a stuck greedy agent just gets a new nearby target, so the controller barely matters. On mixed it is decisive — greedy 31, planner 57, right against the 60 ceiling.

2 · The conv planners reach the upper bound on clutter and mixed. On dense clutter and mixed they sit within a few points of god-view BFS despite planning on a partial belief — the multi-agent collective belief plus per-step replanning recover almost all of what full-map knowledge would buy.

3 · Rooms is the honest weak spot. There the learned planners reach ~42 against a 58 upper bound — a real ~15-point gap. Corridor mazes need the whole map to route, and with sensing radius 1 the gossip reveals corridor structure too slowly. This is the one terrain where the partial belief clearly costs.

4 · MSP is below the lower bound everywhere hard — dense clutter 52.6 < 56.9, rooms 27.7 < 36.6, mixed 26.5 < 31.4. It is worse than no planner at all. Decisive.

The read. Among the value-iteration planners the architecture is invisible (MVProp ≈ GPPN ≈ Highway everywhere); what moves the number is the terrain and the belief. On open-ish clutter the planner is nearly free to skip; on corridor maps it helps but is belief-limited; MSP should be dropped. The leverage remains upstream — richer belief (comms/connectivity) and goal assignment — not the planner family.
How to read the numbers. The L3 here is a god-view Voronoi assigner with no connectivity constraint, so the team shatters (λ₂ low) and these are unconstrained coverage potentials — higher than the connectivity-constrained trained policy achieves. That also reconciles the earlier end-to-end result (trained greedy 28% vs planner 59% on clutter, ~2×): that number folds in the learned L3 and the connectivity tax, where a frozen agent's cost compounds. This bracket deliberately strips those away to isolate the L1 controller.
