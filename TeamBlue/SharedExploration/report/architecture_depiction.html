Why 32² needs the big GPU — it's the activations, not the weights

The weights are <1 MB, so the 50 GB isn't the model. And there is no backprop-through-time — recurrence is feedforward, so each step's forward is independent. One PPO update instead differentiates the whole batch at once and must hold every intermediate activation for a single backward pass:

16 rollouts × 100 steps × N agents = 16,000 forwards (N=10)  ·  activations ≈ rollouts × horizon × N × grid² × width × 4 bytes

24² · 6 agents

576 cells  → peak ≈ ~17 GB  fits gaius (49 GB)

32² · 10 agents

1024 cells  → peak ≈ ~50 GB  needs balthar (98 GB)

The jump is (1024/576) × (10/6) ≈ 3×. Fixes that keep all 16 rollouts and change zero numbers: minibatch the loss (chunk the wide batch, accumulate gradient) or remat (recompute activations in the backward instead of storing). bf16 (halve the bytes) is the fallback if a small numerical change is acceptable.
