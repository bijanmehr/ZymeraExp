Why 32² needs the big GPU — activations, not weights

The weights are <1 MB, so the 50 GB isn't the model. Recurrence is feedforward → no backprop-through-time: one PPO update differentiates the whole batch at once and holds every feature map above for a single backward pass.

16 rollouts × 100 steps × 10 agents = 16,000 forwards  ·  activations ≈ 16,000 × 2·(64·32·32) × 4 B ≈ 8.4 GB for the actor convs alone

24² · 6 agents

576 cells → peak ≈ ~17 GB · fits gaius (49 GB)

32² · 10 agents

1024 cells → peak ≈ ~50 GB · needs balthar (98 GB)

The jump is (1024/576) × (10/6) ≈ 3×. Fixes that keep all 16 rollouts and change zero numbers: minibatch the loss or remat. bf16 is the numeric-change fallback.
