0The pipeline at a glance

Five stages turn one agent's local view into a move; a separate critic (training only) scores the joint state.

1Perception — the CNN encoder

Each agent's 7-channel local picture → two 3×3 convolutions (64 filters) → global average pool → a 64-number feature. 41,088 params

2Communication — the GNN

Each agent's 64-feature is fused with its in-range neighbours' features over the comm graph, twice (message → max-aggregate), producing the 64-number belief. Size-invariant (works at any team size). 41,728 params

3Decision — the L3 heads

Four small linear heads read the 64-d belief. The goal head and role head are the two sampled actions; value and λ̂₂ are readouts for training. 910 params (+ 3,851 for compass/goal-residual)

4Planning & action — L2 planner → L1 move

The chosen goal cell + the agent's known-walls map go to the frozen MVProp planner, which floods a value field; L1 takes the neighbouring move that climbs it, with a collision veto. 19 params (frozen) · 0 in L1

5The CTDE critic — training only

Reads all agents' observations (centralized), encodes each with a shared CNN, mean-pools over agents (so it's count-invariant), and outputs one joint-state value that supplies the advantages. Discarded at deployment. 45,505 params

7Why 32² needs the big GPU

The weights are <1 MB — so the 50 GB isn't the model, it's the activations. Recurrence is feedforward, so there's no backprop-through-time: one PPO update differentiates the whole batch at once and holds every feature map from stages ①–② simultaneously for a single backward pass.

16 rollouts × 100 steps × 10 agents = 16,000 forwards · actor convs ≈ 16,000 × 2·(64·32·32)·4 B ≈ 8.4 GB alone

24² · 6 agents

576 cells → peak ≈ ~17 GB · fits gaius (49 GB)

32² · 10 agents

1024 cells → peak ≈ ~50 GB · needs balthar (98 GB)

The jump is (1024/576)×(10/6) ≈ 3×. Fixes that keep all 16 rollouts and change zero numbers: minibatch the loss or remat; bf16 is the numeric-change fallback.
