Why 32² needs the big GPU — activations, not weights

The weights are under 1 MB, so the 50 GB is not the model — it is the activations. There is no backprop-through-time (the recurrence above is off), so one training update differentiates the whole batch at once and must hold every feature map from perception and the graph in memory at the same time for a single backward pass.

16 rollouts × 100 steps × 10 agents = 16,000 forwards · perception activations ≈ 16,000 × 2·(64·32·32)·4 B ≈ 8.4 GB alone

24² · 6 agents

576 cells → peak ≈ ~17 GB · fits gaius (49 GB)

32² · 10 agents

1024 cells → peak ≈ ~50 GB · needs balthar (98 GB)

The jump is (1024/576)×(10/6) ≈ 3×. Levers that keep all 16 rollouts and change zero numbers: minibatch the loss, or recompute activations in the backward pass; half-precision is the fallback that does change numbers.
