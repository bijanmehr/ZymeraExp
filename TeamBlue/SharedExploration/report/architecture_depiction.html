Why 32² needs the big GPU — activations, not weights

The weights are under 1 MB, so the 50 GB isn't the model — it's the activations. Recurrence is feedforward, so there's no backprop-through-time: one PPO update differentiates the whole batch at once and must hold every feature map from stages ①–② in memory at the same time for a single backward pass.

16 rollouts × 100 steps × 10 agents = 16,000 forwards · actor convs ≈ 16,000 × 2·(64·32·32)·4 B ≈ 8.4 GB alone

24² · 6 agents

576 cells → peak ≈ ~17 GB · fits gaius (49 GB)

32² · 10 agents

1024 cells → peak ≈ ~50 GB · needs balthar (98 GB)

The jump is (1024/576)×(10/6) ≈ 3×. Levers that keep all 16 rollouts and change zero numbers: minibatch the loss or remat; bf16 is the numeric-change fallback.

Parameter counts and shapes are exact — introspected via jax.tree_util over the Equinox modules. The 206,374 trainable total is actor (160,869) + critic (45,505); the frozen distilled MVProp planner (19 params) runs in the forward pass but carries no gradient and is not part of that total. Output shapes assume 32×32 input with same-padding (stride 1, pad 1 preserves spatial size). Param bars share one linear scale (max = the 45,568-param GCRN); zero-parameter layers show an empty track. Activation-memory figures are order-of-magnitude from the dominant CNN + stored-trajectory terms.
