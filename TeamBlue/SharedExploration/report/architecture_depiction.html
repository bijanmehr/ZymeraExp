Why 32² needs the big GPU — activations, not weights

Weights are <1 MB, so the 50 GB is not the model. And there's no backprop-through-time (recurrence is feedforward). One PPO update differentiates the whole batch at once — every feature map above, held simultaneously for a single backward pass:

16 rollouts × 100 steps × N agents = 16,000 forwards  ·  activations ≈ rollouts × horizon × N × grid² × width × 4 B

24² · 6 agents

576 cells → peak ≈ ~17 GB · fits gaius (49 GB)

32² · 10 agents

1024 cells → peak ≈ ~50 GB · needs balthar (98 GB)

The jump is (1024/576)×(10/6) ≈ 3×. Fixes that keep all 16 rollouts and change zero numbers: minibatch the loss (chunk the wide batch, accumulate gradient) or remat (recompute in backward). bf16 is the numeric-change fallback.
