What the runs validly show

  • Scale hurts coverage, as expected. Rooms coverage falls from ~52% at 24²/6 to ~37% at 32²/10 — more space, sparser team. Connectivity margin (mean λ₂) stays similar (~1.9), so the bigger team is not better connected; it just covers less.
  • Terrain sets the regime, not the controller. Open (24²): ~77% cov, λ₂≈3.7, 98–100% connected. Rooms: λ₂ dips below 0.5 on 15–34% of steps — real corridor disconnections the mean-λ₂ constraint never reacts to. (32² open baseline skipped by design.)
  • setpool ≈ setattn at both scales, within seed noise — attention critic adds nothing over mean-pooling.

Recommendation

The mechanism comparison is settled (null): the full rooms cross is complete at both scales, every unit dual=0. The 32² open baseline was deliberately skipped — λ₂≈3.7 there binds even less, so it would only add coverage/λ₂ baselines, no mechanism signal.

Re-run with a binding τ: set the floor above natural mean λ₂ (τ ∈ {1.5, 2.0, 2.5} for rooms; open needs its own higher τ, or τ as a fraction of unconstrained λ₂).

Or switch to a per-step / low-percentile λ₂ constraint — that's what would catch the 15–34% corridor dips a mean constraint ignores.
