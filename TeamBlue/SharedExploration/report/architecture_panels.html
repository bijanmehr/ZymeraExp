Zymera · policy architecture · actor / critic

Two networks: one per agent, one for the team

The actor is the policy — it runs on every agent, with the same shared weights. The critic runs once over the whole team, only during training. Shapes are for a 32×32 grid with 10 agents; every count is exact.

The number that matters for scaling: adding an agent adds zero parameters. The actor's weights are shared by every agent, and the critic averages over agents in a way that does not depend on how many there are — so the system is 206,374 parameters whether the team is 1 agent or 100. What each extra agent adds is compute and memory, not weights. Both panels below are counted; the scaling is worked out underneath.

How a team of N scales — exact numbers

The actor box above is instantiated once per agent, but every copy shares the same weights. The critic sees all agents through one count-invariant pool. So the weight count is flat in N; only the work grows.

The "≈ 5 GB per agent" is not a guess — it falls out of the two measured peaks. At 24×24 with 6 agents the run peaks near 17 GB (2.83 GB/agent); at 32×32 with 10 agents it peaks near 50 GB (5.00 GB/agent). The ratio of those two per-agent numbers is 1.77, which is exactly the grid-area ratio:

(50 / 10) / (17 / 6) = 1.77 ≈ 32² / 24² = 1024 / 576 = 1.78

So the memory model is settled: peak ≈ N × grid² × constant — linear in the number of agents, quadratic in the grid side. That is why 32×32 with 10 agents (~50 GB) needs balthar while 24×24 with 6 agents (~17 GB) fits gaius, even though the two networks have identical weights.

Counts are exact — from the module shapes, reconciled against the introspected actor (160,869) and critic (45,505) totals. The actor's "70,680 active" is the deployed forward path plus two training readouts; the remaining built weights (recurrence, attention query/key, alternative tool-heads) are switched off in this configuration. The 19-parameter MVProp planner is frozen and injected at training time, so it is outside the 206,374 trainable total. Activation figures are the dominant convolutional term; measured peaks include the backward pass and PPO's stored rollouts.
