What you're watching
Left = the KB itself (channel.shared): a fog-of-war map from this agent's point of view. It starts fully dark — no prior knowledge (at t=0 just the single 3×3 patch it can sense from its spawn), and cells light up only as the agent senses them or hears them over the comm graph. Knowledge only grows (OR-gossip); walls too are revealed only once discovered. Right = the same knowledge as the policy reads it — the 64-number encoding z(t) the GNN-KB emits. 64 raw numbers aren't legible, so instead of a heatmap of dimensions we show three human-readable views: its 2D journey (PCA — where the internal state travels), the knowledge it tracks, and ‖Δz‖ (how much it shifts each step).
