Zymera · Survey-grounded decision brief

What the field actually knows about LLM/SLM for cooperative multi-agent RL

Not a paper list — the synthesized verdicts of the review literature, so we can decide what to build first without testing everything.

7-area survey sweep · ~40 review / systematic / benchmark sources · organized by the three questions · 2026-08

Bottom line. The one architecture the whole literature converges on is already the vision: use the LLM/SLM as a slow-clock component that emits a reusable artifact — reward code, a goal/role/region spec, a plan — which a fast RL controller executes, and never query it per control step. What is refuted: putting the model in the loop or expecting capability to emerge from language (per-step LLM control ≈20% on 8-agent MAPF, 0% in mazes; debate loses to a single strong agent at equal budget; small models ≤~13B cannot self-optimize or reliably multi-step-plan — a hard floor). The highest-payoff, lowest-risk first move is LLM-as-reward-designer in program space (Eureka: beat human experts on 83% of 29 tasks, +52% avg), which runs offline on a capable model so the on-device SLM never carries it.
The Zymera-specific catch. Every relevant survey names covert/stealthy internal adversaries + partial-observability/lossy-comms as an open gap, and none operationalize it — genuinely unclaimed territory that maps onto our thesis. But two red lines: (1) distillation silently erodes calibration + on-rollout robustness — the exact properties a covert-adversary defense depends on; (2) higher explanation fidelity ⇒ higher attackability — the explanation channel is itself an attack surface. Co-measure what the small model lost, not just its accuracy.

Try this first — ranked by evidence × fit × low risk

1
LLM-as-reward-designer in program space, run offlineQ3

A capable model writes/edits the coverage-connectivity reward + curriculum as executable code, scored against the real mission rollout, reflect → mutate (Eureka/EoH). Strongest single result in the corpus (83%/+52%), entirely off the fast loop, and offline execution sidesteps the small-model optimization floor. Pitfall: Eureka misaligns when reflection sees only coarse reward stats — feed it the mission-level signal + keep a rule validator rejecting invalid configs.

2
Surrogate-constrained explanation with the on-agent SLMQ2

Distill each agent's fast RL controller into a shallow decision tree/path; the small LM verbalizes that. Best-validated Q2 recipe — ~2× less hallucination, and gains largest on small models (n=29 human study). The one Q2 result that rewards the small-LM premise. Pitfall: it explains the surrogate, not the policy internals — pair with an intervention/StateMask fidelity test from day one; treat any self-report as unverified.

3
Slow-clock SLM emits artifacts (goal region / role / allocation) over the fast RL controller; event-triggeredQ1

Strongest cross-survey consensus and literally our architecture; field-validated for embodied resource-constrained teams. Pitfall: never per-step (control collapses; formation-center bug). Validate on ≤10 agents before scaling — no LLM coordination is demonstrated at swarm scale.

4
Hybrid centralized-plan / decentralized-execute + explicit capability groundingQ1

Central allocator + local validators; ground capability ("robot resumes") to stop assign-to-incapable hallucination. HMAS-2 82.5% vs 25% for fully-decentralized; a constraint-aware small model beat a larger ungrounded one. Pitfall: flat peer-to-peer dialog exhausts context as the team grows — don't default to a flat swarm.

5
LLM warm-start → classical optimizer for any numeric HPOQ3

LM seeds an initial config / prunes the space; TPE/GP-BO does the search. Pitfall: the "win" is the default-config seed, not the model (p=0.92); budget-match against a sensible default first, and never let a small LM be the numeric optimizer.

6
Instrument the covert-adversary regime the surveys name but none testQ1/Q2 · the novel claim

Run a comm-channel ablation early (drop/degrade the channel → measure the coordination cliff); treat covert-micro → macro propagation as the primary robustness target. Survey-certified unclaimed territory that is the Zymera thesis. Pitfall: distillation erodes the calibration/robustness this depends on — co-measure what the SLM lost.

The SLM crux — the capability-vs-size floor

Where the vision lives or dies. The small-model / on-device / distillation surveys give a sharp quantitative verdict.

AxisWhere SLMs are greenWhere they hit the cliff
Capability by size (70-model bench)0.5–3B hit 64–68% commonsense; classification/retrieval/drafting solid on-device; 1–1.5B sweet spot (Apple 3B, Gemini Nano ship)Math / multi-step reasoning collapses below ~2B — the exact ability a coordinator/negotiator needs is the one that degrades most
What data buysData quality dominates parameter count — Qwen2-1.5B beats several 3B; over-train far past ChinchillaChinchilla is the wrong law for device SLMs; no deployment-aware scaling law exists
Distillation — preservesInstruction-following, CoT, tool-use, domain specialization, alignment; a distilled student can exceed its teacher in data-limited regimesCannot conjure emergent abilities absent at student scale — no distilling past the reasoning cliff without parameters
Distillation — silently loses—Calibration, on-rollout robustness (exposure bias), grounding, safety, output diversity. A student looks "smaller" but is actually "narrower, less calibrated, less safe"
Energy → mandates slow clock4-bit weight-only ~free at ≥1B; GQA+RMSNorm+SiLU standard~700 tokens per phone battery; response length is the #1 energy predictor; always-on per-agent LM is not viable
Hardware reality—NPUs lose decode (20–23 vs 51–76 tok/s on CPU); framework choice = up to 15× throughput gap on identical silicon

Make-or-break reading: the energy budget doesn't just permit slow-clock-over-fast-RL — it mandates it (the field's implicit validation of the premise). But the small model cannot be the reasoner of the swarm — it must emit structured artifacts under external verification (the LLM-Modulo pattern: SLM proposes, a connectivity-check/planner verifies, loop on failure). And on-device full-gradient training is off the table (Adam ≈12× inference memory); field adaptation means LoRA/QLoRA or zeroth-order MeZO only.
